Tune Lab Privacy Policy

한국어 · Applies to version 1.0.3 (22) · Effective November 28, 2026

Tune Lab (com.ppumppum.tunelab) is an Android app for music editing and practice available on Google Play under the developer name 뿜뿜. This policy describes how the app and its operators handle your files and information.

1. Information we handle

The app processes the audio and videos you select, your recordings, media details such as file names and durations, editing projects, analysis and sheet music results, and settings on your device. AI inference runs on your device. The app does not upload your audio, recordings, videos, sheet music, lyrics, chat prompts, responses, or analysis results to our servers or to an external AI service.

  • Advanced AI limits and rewards. When you use these features, Game Services issues a guest identifier without a sign-up screen. The app stores a recovery token on your device. The server processes the guest identifier, the feature used, the usage event ID, the app version and time of use, and ad ticket and reward status to manage free uses, deliver rewards, and prevent duplicate rewards. It may use your IP address to limit excessive requests.
  • Rewarded ads. Google AdMob can process device and app identifiers such as the advertising ID, the connection IP address and approximate location inferred from it, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse. When you request an ad reward, the app passes a guest identifier and a ticket to AdMob to verify the reward. How Google uses information from partner apps.
  • Request verification. The app uses Play Integrity for advanced AI usage records and ad ticket requests. Google checks the app version and signature, Play licensing status, and device integrity signals. The server uses the result to verify requests. Play Integrity data handling.

2. Permissions and your choices

  • Audio, storage, and video. These permissions let you select media for editing and extract audio from a video.
  • Microphone. When you start recording, the app saves the recording on your device. When you tap the microphone for a voice command or dictation, the app uses Android speech recognition. It tries on-device recognition first. If that is unavailable, the default recognition service may send your speech to its own servers depending on your device settings.
  • Notifications and background work. These allow tasks you started to continue outside the current screen and show their progress or completion.
  • System settings. Used only when you set a sound you cut as your ringtone, notification sound, or alarm.

You can change permissions in Android settings. You can use screen controls or the keyboard instead of voice input. The app does not request location permission. Android settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID. The ad SDK may present a consent screen depending on your region.

3. Model downloads

The app downloads AI models through Google Play Asset Delivery. It downloads the AI chat model from Hugging Face over HTTPS, which can receive the IP address of the download request. Model download requests do not include audio, prompts, or analysis results. Google Privacy Policy · Hugging Face Privacy Policy.

4. Retention and deletion

Work files, analysis results, project history, and settings are stored on your device. You can remove local data using the app's deletion features, by clearing app storage in Android, or by uninstalling the app. The guest recovery token is stored in Android Block Store so the app can recover your limits and benefits after reinstallation.

The server deletes advanced AI usage records after 90 days. Ad tickets that never led to a reward are deleted within a day after they expire. Records of rewards already given are kept so the same ad cannot be rewarded twice. The guest identifier and other server information used to verify rewards are kept as needed to provide the features, recover access, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. You can contact us about access, correction, or deletion of guest data. Uninstalling does not automatically delete server data.

Original files and files you export to shared folders may remain after you uninstall. When you export or share files with another app or a cloud provider, that service's policy applies.

5. Security and contact

The app uses Android's access controls for its internal storage. Game Services, model downloads, and ad SDK communications use encryption in transit. Speech transmissions and retention follow the selected Android recognition service's policy. If you email us, we process your address and message to respond and may retain them as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

For privacy inquiries, including access, correction, or deletion requests, contact Tune Lab support (itkong) at ponykit@gmail.com.

6. Children and policy updates

Tune Lab is a general-audience music editing and practice tool. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us at the address above for children's privacy inquiries. We update this page when our data practices change.
